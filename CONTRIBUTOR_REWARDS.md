# Contributor rewards — a statement of intent

**This is a statement of intent, not an offer, a contract or a promise of any
amount.** It exists so that the intent is written down before there is money
to talk about, and so that it can be held against the project later.

## The intent

ModelMRI's methods are free, and the project plans to fund itself by selling
the operating layer around them — ModelMRI Cloud / Enterprise, described in
[LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md). If that succeeds, the project intends to set
aside part of that commercial success as a **contributor reward pool**, and to
pay it out to the people whose contributions made the difference.

There is no percentage, no formula and no schedule yet. Naming one before
there is revenue, an entity, and tax and legal advice would be a number nobody
could check, which is the one kind of number this project does not publish.

## Principles

- **Impact over volume.** What counts is what a contribution changed for the
  people who use the tool — a method that works, a measurement that stopped
  lying, an adapter that opened a family of models — not lines, commits or
  activity.
- **The founder is excluded.** The Project Lead does not draw from the pool.
  It is for contributors.
- **Transparent, published allocations.** When the pool pays out, who received
  what, and the reasoning, is published. A reward nobody can see is not a
  reward the project can be held to.
- **GitHub is evidence, not the judge.** The repository's history is the record
  of who did what; it is not a scoreboard, and no metric derived from it
  decides an allocation on its own. People decide, in the open, and say why.

## What this is not

Participation in ModelMRI — contributing, reviewing, maintaining, or being
named anywhere in this repository — **creates no employment, equity,
partnership, ownership, or revenue right of any kind**, and nothing here
entitles anyone to a payment. The pool, when it exists, is a discretionary
allocation from the project, made under whatever legal and tax framework the
project has at the time.

This document is deliberately separate from the [Contributor License
Agreement](CLA.md) and from the software licenses. Contributing is governed
by those; rewards are not a term of either, and no license or agreement in
this repository refers to this document as a condition or a consideration.

## Machinery, deferred

Labels for reward-relevant work, a ledger of allocations, and any automation
around them will be built when the project has regular external contributors
to reward, and not before. Until then this page is the whole mechanism: an
intent, four principles, and a paragraph saying what it is not.
