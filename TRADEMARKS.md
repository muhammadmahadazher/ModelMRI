# Trademarks — draft policy

**Status: draft, pending legal review.** It states the project's intent and
the uses it considers fine and not fine; it is not legal advice.

## The marks

"**ModelMRI**" and the ModelMRI logo are **unregistered** marks of the
ModelMRI project. No registration is claimed and the registered-trademark
symbol is not used; the project intends to check availability and register
the name once it has a legal entity, and this page will say so when that has
happened. Until then the
marks are used as the project's name and identity, and this page asks that
they be treated as such.

## What the software licenses do and do not grant

The AGPL-3.0-only and Apache-2.0 licenses in this repository grant copyright
and patent rights to the code. **Neither grants a right to use the project's
name or logo** to imply affiliation, endorsement or origin — the Apache
License says so in its section 6, and the AGPL grants no such right at all.
You can fork ModelMRI, modify it, run it and redistribute it under the terms
of its licenses; what you cannot do is call the result ModelMRI in a way that
makes people think it came from here.

## Fine

- Saying that something is **"based on ModelMRI"**, **"built with ModelMRI"**,
  **"compatible with ModelMRI"**, or **"a fork of ModelMRI"**, truthfully.
- **`.mri` interoperability claims**: "writes `.mri` files", "reads `.mri`
  files", "opens in the ModelMRI viewer". The format is meant to be used
  everywhere, and saying so is encouraged.
- Referring to ModelMRI by name in articles, papers, talks, comparisons,
  reviews, tutorials and package descriptions.
- Naming a package, adapter or plugin so that its relationship is clear —
  "`something-for-modelmri`", "a ModelMRI adapter for X" — as long as it does
  not present itself as an official part of ModelMRI.
- Using the name and logo unmodified to link to the project.

## Not fine

- Presenting a fork, a modified build, or a hosted service **as ModelMRI**, as
  "the official ModelMRI", or in a way that suggests the project runs,
  endorses or supports it. A modified version should carry its own name, or
  say clearly and prominently that it is an unofficial modification.
- Using the name or logo as, or as part of, the name or logo of your own
  product, service or company.
- Altering the logo, or combining it with other marks so that the result reads
  as a new mark.
- Registering "ModelMRI", or anything confusingly similar, as a trademark,
  domain name, account name or package name in a way that suggests it is the
  project's own.

## Questions

If a use is not covered here, or you are not sure, open a GitHub issue and
say what you have in mind. Permission for a use that this page does not
already allow is given in writing or not at all.
