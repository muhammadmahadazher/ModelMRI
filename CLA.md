# Contributor License Agreement — DRAFT

**Status: draft, pending legal review. Not in force.** Nobody is asked to sign
this yet. Until counsel has reviewed it and the project starts asking for it —
which happens when the first pull request from outside arrives, and not
before — the inbound rule in [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md) is what
applies: a contribution is licensed under the license of the files it
changes.

## In plain English

- **You keep your copyright.** This is a license, not an assignment. You can
  use your own contribution anywhere else, under any terms, exactly as if you
  had never sent it here.
- **ModelMRI may include your work in ModelMRI Community and in commercially
  licensed distributions.** The commercial license is how the project intends
  to be funded (see [LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md)); this agreement is what makes
  it possible to offer one that covers contributed code.
- **Your accepted work stays open.** Whatever else the project does with it,
  it promises to also keep licensing your contribution under the license the
  project was using for that code on the day you submitted it — AGPL-3.0-only
  for the application, Apache-2.0 for the packages. That promise is the second
  sentence of section 2.3 below, and it is the reason this agreement uses the
  Harmony "Option Five" rather than a plain assignment.
- **You confirm you have the right to contribute**, and you disclose anything
  in your contribution that you did not write or do not own — the provenance
  rule in CONTRIBUTING.md is the same rule in fewer words.
- There are two forms: one for individuals, one for entities (an employer, a
  company). Read the one that applies to you.

## What is filled in, what is not

The text below is the Harmony Contributor License Agreement, version 1.0, in
its Individual (CLA-I) and Entity (CLA-E) forms, reproduced verbatim; only
formatting is ours. Harmony offers five alternatives for section 2.3 and
expects the adopter to keep one — **Option Five** is kept and Options One to
Four are omitted, which is the selection Harmony's own configurator makes.
The template's variables are handled as follows:

| Variable | Here |
|---|---|
| `[PROJECT_NAME]`, "We", "Us" | ModelMRI. Today that means Muhammad Mahad Azher personally, the project's sole copyright holder; when the project has a legal entity, the entity. This is one of the items for counsel. |
| `[LIST_OF_MEDIA_LICENSES]` | CC-BY-4.0, the license the project uses for documentation and media (see LICENSING.md). |
| `[SUBMISSION_INSTRUCTIONS]` | **Not set.** The intent is electronic acceptance on your first pull request, through a CLA bot, once this agreement is in force. |
| `[NONOWNER_INSTRUCTIONS]` | **Not set.** The intent is the provenance rule in CONTRIBUTING.md: name what you did not write, where it came from, and under what license. |
| `[JURISDICTION]` | **Not set.** Counsel decides, together with the entity question. |
| `[or Your Affiliates]` (Individual form, 2.2) | Left as the template has it, for counsel. |

Nothing in this draft is legal advice.
